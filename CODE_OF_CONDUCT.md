# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accountability, and protecting personal and network data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, doxxing, publication of private data, deliberate privacy-boundary violations, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code. Enforcement decisions should avoid further disclosure of private information.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers through a private route listed on the repository profile. Do not include unnecessary personal data in the initial report. Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, protect reporter privacy as far as practical, and disclose conflicts of interest.

Maintainers may apply correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal according to impact and pattern of behavior.
